# Director Intake and Creative Baseline

Use this file when the script needs a deeper director-preproduction pass before final asset prompts.

## Fact Levels

Separate every important production claim into one of four levels:

- `剧本事实`: explicitly stated in the script or confirmed reference.
- `强推断`: supported by multiple script clues; cite the clues briefly.
- `导演提案`: not written, but production needs one concrete choice for country, era, technology, architecture, costume culture, social class, language, lighting, or world rules.
- `待定冲突`: a choice that would materially change assets, continuity, or story meaning.

Do not disguise a proposal as script fact. After user confirmation, the proposal becomes project truth.

## Deep Director Breakdown

For full-package requests, compile these items before final prompts:

1. One-sentence story and emotional theme: protagonist, goal, threat, transformation.
2. Genre and realism level: main genre, subgenre, live-action vs stylized ratio.
3. Country, region, era: enough specificity to constrain technology, clothing, architecture, transport, and communication.
4. Season and story timeline: weather, day/night pattern, elapsed time, light change.
5. Language and sound culture: dialogue language, accent, class/education influence, speaking rhythm.
6. World and threat rules: supernatural/technology limits, damage, death, ambiguity.
7. Spatial chain: the major spaces crossed by the plot and how one space leads to another.
8. Character relationship and mini-bible: age range, social role, relationship conflict, fear/lack, arc, posture, skin/hair/life marks, voice, and asset dependencies.
9. State chain and visual motifs: costume changes, injuries, mutation, prop handoffs, recurring colors, repeated objects, light changes, destroyed/intact states.
10. Optional emotion curve: use `emotion-curve.md` when requested or when a visual rhythm map would guide production.

Keep the analysis concise. When the user asks for prompt-only output, compress these items into assumptions and visual DNA instead of showing a long essay.

## Creative Baseline

When style or production quality matters, propose one coherent baseline instead of multiple vague options:

- One primary film/show/photography reference only when it is useful; name exactly what is borrowed: light, color, lens distance, texture, scale, atmosphere, or performance distance.
- A camera/medium choice: either real film stock or a specific digital-cinema look; do not claim both as the capture source.
- Lens family, aspect ratio, and frame rate when video or storyboard planning is requested.
- Light and exposure rule: source, direction, diffusion, contrast, highlight rolloff, shadow behavior, and what may disappear into darkness.
- Color strategy: environment color, supporting colors, saturation, black level, middle tones, skin protection, forbidden colors.
- Material and atmosphere: fabric, metal, jade, wood, stone, dust, rain, fog, breath, skin, hair.
- Sound/music bible only when useful: environmental sound, action sound, non-verbal human sound, BGM function, instruments, frequency range, silence, and forbidden sound language.

Every style claim must translate into visible or audible parameters. Remove empty phrases such as “高级感,” “很电影,” or “有质感” unless they are backed by concrete choices.

## Confirmation Gate

Ask for confirmation only when:

- a `待定冲突` would change assets or continuity;
- dependency order requires an upstream character/costume/reference to be accepted;
- the user requested an explicit staged production workflow.

Otherwise proceed with labeled assumptions.
